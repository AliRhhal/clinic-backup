Clinic Backup

A private tool for one dental clinic. It stores the clinic's encrypted database backups and software update files in the clinic's own Google Drive account. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

The tool only accesses files it created itself in the clinic's Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see other files in the account. This data stays in the clinic's Google account and isn't sold or shared with anyone.
